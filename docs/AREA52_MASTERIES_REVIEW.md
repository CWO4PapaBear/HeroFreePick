# Area 52 Masteries review

Reviewed local Area 52 Spell.dbc and CharacterAdvancement.dbc. These records establish client descriptions and membership, not current live server behavior. No addon behavior was changed.

Found 41 relevant mastery descriptions: 23 have advancement records and 18 are spell-only matches. Of the 23, 21 explicitly say learning a group ability automatically grants its mastery, letting other members cost 1 Ability Essence and no Ability Gems.

Presence Mastery states no gem cost but does not specify 1 Essence; alternate advancement records cost 2 and 4 AE. Seal Mastery describes draft-mode availability and no gem cost, without the same automatic/1-Essence rule. The 18 spell-only records instead describe free automatic unlocks at Class Point requirements; their availability remains unverified.

Membership below comes from explicit spell links in each description, not inferred class/category membership. The wording does not establish refunds, first-purchase repricing, removal behavior, or server-side calculations.
