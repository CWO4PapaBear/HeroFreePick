# Mage travel destination schedule

Integration specification, not a deployed spell package. 160 Rune destinations are included, each with a planned teleport and portal (320 spells). Acherus is excluded by request. Faction and other restrictions still determine which a character can use.

Faction counts: 56 Alliance, 57 Horde, 47 neutral.

Levels below are the proposed zone progression policy. A capital is level 10 for its listed races and 15 for other races of its faction. Ratchet/Booty Bay are 25, Shattrath 62 and Dalaran 72. Other starter settlements begin at 15 to preserve racial-capital-only access at level 10.

The JSON alongside this report preserves exact Rune arrival coordinates, phase, reputation and structured quest restrictions. Rune IDs are references only; separate Mage IDs must be allocated before implementation.
