# Portal and Teleport Mastery progression — 0.39.0

Tracking Mastery stays level 1. Portal Mastery and Teleport Mastery are independent level-10 purchases, each 2 AP and one Uncommon gem. Teleport Mastery uses Teleport: Dalaran artwork. Classic remains unchanged.

| Level | Each Mastery's eligible destinations |
|---:|---|
| 10 | Racial capital (Gnomes share Ironforge; Trolls share Orgrimmar). |
| 15 | Four capitals of the actual character faction. |
| 25 | Ratchet and Booty Bay. |
| 62 | Shattrath. |
| 72 | Dalaran. |

The addon currently contains 13 members per Mastery, including separate faction Shattrath variants. Portal Mastery grants only portals; Teleport Mastery grants only teleports. Buying or removing one does not buy or remove the other. Existing Portal owners must select Teleport Mastery separately to keep teleport preview grants.

The expanded 160-destination schedule, faction restrictions and new reagent-production behavior are recorded in HERO-TRAVEL-INTEGRATION-PLAN.md. Acherus is excluded. The remaining destinations and independent Mage spell definitions are not yet implemented. Current neutral previews reference Rune IDs; selecting them does not unlock Rune ownership. No stock/Rune reagent behavior has been changed by the addon.
